How to read this guide

These are real 2026 ranges for custom builds in Sullivan County, NY, based on actual project costs. Every build is different, but if a builder won't show you numbers in these ranges, ask why.

Part One

Land: $50K to $500K+

The lot sets the ceiling for everything after it.

Wooded interior lots, 2 to 10 acres $50K to $150K
Elevated lots with long views $150K to $300K
Lakefront or significant water frontage $250K to $500K+

What moves a lot up in price

Views, water, privacy (no visible neighbors), southern exposure, existing driveway or cleared areas.

Part Three

Construction cost per square foot, by finish level

This is the core of the guide. Three tiers:

Quality custom

$300 to $400 per sqft

Real hardwood or high-end LVP, quartz counters, quality appliances, good windows, thoughtful lighting. A genuinely nice home. Most production-adjacent custom builds land here.

High-end

$450 to $600 per sqft

Wide-plank floors, stone counters, premium appliance packages, custom millwork in key rooms, better window packages, landscape lighting, outdoor living space designed in from the start.

Luxury

$650 to $900+ per sqft

Everything above, plus: full custom millwork, stone or exotic tile throughout, professional-grade kitchens, spa bathrooms, smart home infrastructure, walls of glass, complex rooflines, high-end exterior materials (cedar, stone, standing seam).

Part Five

What drives cost up the most, ranked

  1. Finish level. The jump from quality to luxury finishes can double the per-sqft number.
  2. Structural complexity. Every corner, cantilever, and glass wall costs money.
  3. Site conditions. Rock, slope, and wetlands show up in site work, not in the per-sqft quote. Always ask about them separately.
  4. Changes mid-build. The most expensive words in construction are "while we're at it."
