How the audit works

An audit is not a repair visit. It is a careful look at your whole home, turned into a plan you can act on.

1

Send photos

Send a few photos of your home and your to-do list, and Joaquin will start from there.

2

Get a flat fee

One flat fee for the audit visit, quoted after the photo intake. No labor or materials are included, because no repairs happen on the visit.

3

Walkthrough visit

Joaquin walks the home, photographs everything, and notes what needs attention.

4

Written report

You get a written report with a prioritized list. Bigger issues are referred to licensed trades.
