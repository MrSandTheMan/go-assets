Meet Socrates

Socrates was born in the Dominican Republic and has been a New Yorker since 1989. Before real estate, he spent more than twenty years as a creative director in branding, marketing, and publishing, work that taught him how a home should be presented, priced, and talked about.

Today he brings that same eye to every client: careful positioning for sellers, clear-eyed guidance for buyers, and negotiation grounded in facts, not pressure. He lives in the Lower East Side with his wife and two young children, which is why home is not an abstraction to him. It is where his own life happens.

His belief is simple. Real estate is about people and goals first, and property second. The right home is the one that fits the life you are building, and the right process is one where you always know where you stand.
