How it works

From first sketch to keys

579 Shore Drive ran 16 months. Plan on 12 to 20 from first sketch to keys: 3 to 6 months for design and permits, 8 to 14 for construction. Here is the road, phase by phase, straight from our Dirt to Dream diary.

1

Design & permits

3 to 6 months

Plans, engineering, septic design, and permits. Slow on paper, and it saves ten times the time in the field.

2

Site work & foundation

~2 months

Clearing, driveway, well, septic, and the foundation pour. The lot becomes a building site.

3

Framing

~2 months

Walls up, roof on. The house takes shape in weeks, and it is the most dramatic phase to watch.

4

Rough-ins

~2 months

Plumbing, electric, HVAC, and insulation. Invisible work that decides how the house lives.

5

Finishes

3 to 4 months

Sheetrock, floors, millwork, tile, counters, fixtures, paint. The house becomes a home.

6

Final stretch

1 to 2 months

Punch list, inspections, certificate of occupancy, deep clean, walkthrough. Then keys.

FAQ

Questions every buyer asks

How much does it cost to build a custom home in Sullivan County?
Construction runs about $300 to $400 per square foot at a quality custom level, $450 to $600 high-end, and $650 to $900+ for luxury. Land typically adds $50K to $500K+ and site work $40K to $200K depending on the lot. As an all-in figure, a $650K build budget typically lands around $775K to $900K once land and site work are added. Our free Cost Guide breaks down every number.
How long does a build take, start to finish?
Plan on 12 to 20 months from first sketch to move-in: roughly 3 to 6 months for design and permits, then 8 to 14 months of construction depending on size and complexity.
How does your pricing work?
Open book, cost-plus. You see the actual costs of your build, and our fee is one fixed number, agreed in writing before you sign. No padded line items, no mystery allowances.
What's included in a Corestone build?
Everything in the "What's included" section above: design, permits, site work, structure, systems, interiors, and finishes, all under one contract with one team. If it is on that list, it is in your price.
I don't own land yet. Where do I start?
Start with the lot, because the lot sets the ceiling for everything after it. Download our Land Buyer's Checklist, and before you commit to a parcel, talk to us. A second set of eyes on perc, well, setbacks, and access can save you from the most expensive mistake in the whole process.
Do you take on renovations?
Selectively. Our focus is custom home builds, development, and commercial work, and we take on major renovations and rebuilds when the project fits. Tell us about yours and we will give you a straight answer.
Where do you build?
Sullivan County and Orange County, NY, and the surrounding Upstate region. Our office is at 1619 NY-52 in Liberty.
